Enterprise Hardening

Bring your product. We'll harden it for your threat model.

Enterprises can engage BLA for a full-cycle hardening engagement: assess release exposure, apply custom defensive layers, and verify delivery. The goal is to raise reverse-engineering cost and reduce the exposed surface—not to promise that anything is unbreakable.

01 · Assess

Architecture & threat-model review

Map your artifacts, build pipeline, and attacker model before choosing intensity and formats.

02 · Custom harden

Tailored protection for your stack

Apply BLA hardening across JS/Electron/WASM/Python/native/models and related release artifacts, tuned to your architecture—not a one-size-fits-all packer.

03 · Verify & deliver

Checks you can ship with

Runtime-equivalence minded verification, defense gates, and a clear report of what was hardened and what remains out of scope.

Honest scope

No absolute security promises

BLA raises automated reverse-engineering cost and plants decoys; patient interactive human reverse engineering remains possible. Engagements are scoped after reviewing your architecture and threat model.
